Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy covers this website, purchases of NetGuard Marine keys and the NetGuard Marine program. Questions: Telegram @kirk_ro.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. We use no cookies, analytics or trackers, and the pages load no third-party fonts.
  • GitHub, as the host, may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • When you press a Buy button, the page loads Paddle's checkout script from Paddle.

Purchases

  • Payments are processed by Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for our orders. Your card or wallet details go to Paddle, not to us. See the Paddle privacy notice.
  • Paddle shares with us what we need to fulfil the order: your name, email address, country and the order details.
  • We use this only to send your key, answer support questions, handle refunds and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell it or pass it to anyone else.
  • We keep order records for as long as support, refunds and legal record-keeping need them.
  • If you write to us on Telegram, we see your Telegram profile and your messages.

The program

  • No telemetry and no account. The program does not report usage and does not check keys online.
  • Licence binding. To tie the trial and the key to one computer, the program reads these identifiers: the Windows installation ID (MachineGuid), the motherboard make and model, the computer make and model, the processor model and the serial number of the system volume (drive C:). It stores only a SHA-256 fingerprint of them, from which the values cannot be recovered, and only on this computer: in %ProgramData%\NetGuard\licence.dat with a copy in the registry (HKLM\SOFTWARE\NetGuard). The command NetGuard.exe --forget-licence erases it.
  • Traffic data stays on the computer. To do its job the program sees which programs use the network, how much traffic they spend and which addresses they connect to. The usage history (90 days) is kept in %ProgramData%\NetGuard.
  • When the program goes online. Once a day it asks GitHub (github.com) whether a new version is out, about 10 KB; you can turn this off in its settings. It downloads an update, measures DNS latency or opens a web page only when you press the button for it.

Your rights

You can ask us what order data we hold about you, ask us to correct it or to delete what the law does not require us to keep. Write to us on Telegram. For payment data, contact Paddle through paddle.net.
