What it does

Every megabyte has an owner.

NetGuard shows which program spends traffic and lets you decide before the money is gone.

Blocking before the download

  • Windows Update, BITS, Delivery Optimization and telemetry
  • Launcher patch servers: games and matchmaking keep working
  • Update domains, through NetGuard's own block in hosts

Modes

  • Satellite Save: maximum saving with one button
  • Game isolation: only the game and programs you marked
  • Strict whitelist and Economy

Unexpected usage

  • Large downloads and speed bursts are held until you decide
  • Idle programs are frozen
  • Background leak and anomaly detectors

Quotas and money

  • A daily quota with warnings and a kill switch
  • Quotas and speed limits for single programs
  • Spending shown in money at your tariff; 90 days of history

Networks and DNS

  • Ship, port, home and hotspot profiles, switched automatically
  • DNS optimizer: thirteen public servers, encrypted DNS
  • Emergency reset brings the internet back with one button

Honest by design

  • No licence checks online and no telemetry
  • One update check a day to GitHub, about 10 KB, can be turned off
  • Everything it changes is put back on removal
